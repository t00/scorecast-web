The Setup

Two devices. One match.

No network. No accounts. No friction. Pair over Bluetooth, pick your format, and start scoring.

Score taps, serves, and game breaks with one-handed control.

Link over BLE

Pair instantly — no Wi-Fi, no pairing codes, no cloud.

Built for the Sport

Everything table tennis
needs to score a match.

From pickup games to club matches — the ITTF rules are baked in.

ITTF rule engine

First to 11, win by 2, serve-every-two, deuce, expedite system, best of 3/5/7 — all handled automatically.

Singles & doubles

Doubles mode tracks server rotation and receiver flip between games automatically.

Bluetooth Low Energy

Direct device-to-device link. Works anywhere — no internet, no router, no latency.

Undo any point

Wrong point? One tap rewinds. Full point log preserves serve order, side swaps, and game state.

Side swaps

End-of-game swaps, mid-game 5-point swaps in the decider, and manual flips — all in one tap.

Handicap matches

Give a head start to balance skill gaps — fair games for mixed-ability players.

Team matches

Run a full Swaythling / Corbillon team format with rotating rubbers and cumulative score.

Multilingual

Full runtime language switching — no reload, no reinstall. English, German and Polish, with more to come.

Spoken scores

Remote announces every point in your chosen language and voice — pick from any voice your device supports.

UK league import

Pull current-season teams and players straight from ttleagues.com — no manual entry, just pick and play.

Privacy by default

All data stays on-device. No accounts, no tracking, no cloud sync unless you share a match.

The BLE Link

Instant pair.
Zero latency.

SCORE-CAST uses Bluetooth Low Energy to beam scores directly from host to remote — no Wi-Fi, no cloud, no pairing codes. Open the app on both devices and they find each other.

Side swaps happen in sync. Undo on the host rewinds the remote. It just works.
